How it works

Pick a publication you love.Everyone it recommends goes into its haystack.
Turn on the Haystack feed.Your Substack Home shows just haystack writers, newest first.
Open the Stackyard.One page of every post and Note your haystacks recommend.

Install (about a minute)

  1. Download the zip and unzip it. Move the Stackyard folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. Chrome runs it from that folder, so don't delete it.
  2. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Return. (In Brave, it's brave://extensions.)
  3. Turn on Developer mode with the switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the Stackyard folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Stackyard, so the little haystack sits in your toolbar.

Chrome may show a note about developer-mode extensions. That's normal for anything installed outside the Chrome Web Store. Updating later: unzip the new version over the old folder, then click the reload arrow on the Stackyard card in chrome://extensions.
